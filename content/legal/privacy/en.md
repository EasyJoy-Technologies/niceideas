## 2. Information we collect

**Account and profile.** When you sign in with NiceMatrix ID, we receive your NiceMatrix ID account identifier and username, and your name and profile picture if you have set them. We use them to create your profile: handle, display name and avatar, plus the bio you write. We do not receive your password, email address or phone number.

**Content you publish.** Posts (text, titles, images and audio), their earlier versions, post settings (such as whether comments are allowed), where each post came from (the web, NiceNote or another app) and when it was published or changed. Images are re-encoded when uploaded, which removes embedded metadata such as location (EXIF). Audio files are stored as uploaded, including any information embedded in them.

**Interactions.** Likes, comments, follows and reports. If you comment without an account, we store your comment and the optional nickname you enter.

**Technical and security information.** To prevent abuse, we create keyed one-way codes ("hashes") from a random browser identifier and from your IP address, and store these codes with likes, comments and reports instead of the IP address itself. Our web server keeps access logs with IP address, time, requested page and browser type. Our network provider tells us the country a request comes from; we use it only to apply the regional rules in Section 8 and do not store it with your profile or content. To deter automated spam, your browser solves a small computational check when you comment without an account; we do not use an third-party CAPTCHA.

**Sign-in sessions.** When you sign in on the web, we store a session record: a one-way hash of the session code, your account identifier, and when the session was created, last used and will expire.

**Information from NiceMatrix ID.** When you request deletion of your NiceMatrix ID, cancel that request, or the deletion is completed, NiceMatrix ID notifies niceideas so that we can act on it.

**Information from apps you authorise.** Apps you authorise send us the posts and media you publish through them, together with the app's identifier.

**What we do not collect.** We do not use advertising, analytics or social-media tracking tools, build advertising profiles or collect precise location. We do not ask for sensitive information, but your posts may reveal it if you choose to publish it.

## 4. Public information

Your profile (handle, display name, avatar, bio, join date, numbers of posts, followers, accounts followed and likes received), your posts and your comments are public. Anyone can see them, including people without an account; search engines may index them; and other platforms may show link previews of them. The nickname on a visitor's comment is public. The codes derived from IP addresses and browser identifiers are never shown.

Posts you make visible only to you are not public: only you can see them, and their images and audio are not publicly accessible while they stay that way.

## 5. Cookies

We use only cookies that the Service needs to work. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `ni_sid` | Keeps you signed in. Our servers store only a hash of it. | 30 days, renewed while you use the Service |
| `ni_oidc` | Protects the sign-in process | 10 minutes |
| `ni_sso` | Remembers that automatic sign-in was already tried, to avoid repeated redirects | 24 hours |

If you delete these cookies, you are signed out and treated as a new visitor.

## 10. Deleting your account

niceideas accounts are deleted by deleting your NiceMatrix ID:

1. When you request deletion, your niceideas profile and posts go offline immediately.
2. If you cancel during the NiceMatrix ID cooling-off period (currently 15 days), everything returns at the same links.
3. When the cooling-off period ends, we permanently delete your profile, posts, earlier versions, media, comments, likes and follows, without any further retention period, apart from the backup cycle of up to 14 days.

You can also delete individual posts at any time, or write to us to delete anything else.
