# v1.0.72 — 2026-10-26

F22 from the sharing review: content taken offline leaves the Cloudflare edge at once (plan `docs/plan/2026-10-26-share-review-fixes.md`, F22; Xianglin 2026-10-26).

## Fixed
- Media and share cards are cached at the Cloudflare edge for an hour; until now a withdrawn image could stay reachable at its old URL for up to that hour. Now:
  - media leaving the public store (post deleted, taken down, edited without them, account deletion) have every rendition URL purged;
  - posts going offline (deleted, taken down, account cooling-off or deletion) have their share-card images purged.
- Purges are queued in the transaction that took the content offline and sent by the worker (≤ 30 URLs per request); failures are retried on the outbox schedule for 24 hours, then dropped with a log line.

## Configuration
- `CLOUDFLARE_PURGE_TOKEN` (Cache Purge on the zone; set on staging and production by Xianglin 2026-10-26). Unset → nothing is queued. The zone is found from the token (host, then parent domains); no zone id to configure.
