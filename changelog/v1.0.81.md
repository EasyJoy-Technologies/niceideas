# v1.0.81 — 2026-10-27

Reposts and quotes, profile tabs Replies / Reposts / Bookmarks (plan `docs/plan/2026-10-27-repost-profile-tabs.md` items 2 and 7, round 2; Xianglin 2026-10-27 12:06 / 12:29: repost hidden in mainland China, Replies and Reposts public on profiles, quotes in All and Reposts, `/bookmarks` stays a page of its own).

## Added
- Repost action (after comment: like · comment · repost · bookmark · share) on cards and post pages (action row and phone bar; the phone-bar menu opens upwards). Menu: Repost / Undo repost, and Quote. Count = plain reposts + live quotes; reposted = green. Signed out: link to sign in; not shown in mainland China or on private posts. `POST /api/web/posts/repost` (same-origin form `slug`, `on`, `back`; 303 back, or JSON `{ reposted, repostCount }` with `Accept: application/json` — interact.js updates in place; 401 signed out, 451 mainland China, 404 private / unknown, 410 offline; bans apply).
- Home timeline: posts the followed accounts reposted, with "X reposted" ("You reposted" for your own); every post once, at its newest occurrence (publishing or newest repost), keyset cursor on that time. Discover and search show no reposts.
- Quotes: Quote opens the compose box (`/?quote=<slug>#compose`, phones: the sheet) with the quoted post full width under the editor, "Add your comment…", short post only (no type switch); the quote is a short post with `quote_of`, shown with the quoted post as an embedded card (author, text or article title / summary, cover thumbnail; links to it) on cards and its page; "This post is no longer available" once the quoted post is private, offline, removed or purged. A quoted post that is gone when posting → message `quote_gone`.
- Notifications: "{name} reposted your post", "{name} quoted your post" (links to the quote).
- Profile tabs (equal width): All · Articles · Replies · Reposts, plus Bookmarks on your own profile (`?tab=bookmarks` elsewhere falls back to All). Replies: the account's comments on public live posts, newest first — "Replying to @x", the text, the post as an embedded card; the card opens the comment. Reposts: plain reposts and quotes, newest first. Own profile: Settings button instead of Follow; Settings page: Sign out (phones had no sign-out outside post pages).
- Privacy policy (40 locales), "Interactions": reposts are public; the profile lists reposts, quotes and comments written while signed in.
- UI texts (40 locales): `share.action.repost`, `share.action.undoRepost`, `share.action.quote`, `share.post.repostedBy`, `share.post.repostedByYou`, `share.quote.unavailable`, `compose.quotePlaceholder`, `compose.error.quote_gone`, `notifications.repost`, `notifications.quote`, `feed.tab.replies`, `feed.tab.reposts`, `feed.tab.bookmarks`.
