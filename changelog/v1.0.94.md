## Changes
- **Fifth visibility level, "Anyone with the link" (`link`):**
  - Anyone with the link opens the post, signed out too: page, media, likes, comments, bookmarks, reports. Reposts and quotes are refused, as for followers / friends posts.
  - Listed only on the author's own profile, with a 🔗 mark, and counted there. Not listed on others' view of the profile (list and count), home timelines (the author's own included, like only-me posts), Discover, search, the post-page aside, quote lists or other people's Replies tab.
  - Lists of posts the viewer already opened keep it: their bookmarks, and reply notifications for comments they wrote there. Link quotes do not notify the quoted author.
  - Post page: a note "This post isn't listed anywhere. Only people with the link can see it." The share card is kept for chat previews (WeChat thumbnail included), but the page has `noindex` and no canonical.
  - Media stay in the private store, served through `/p/{slug}/media/…` to everyone (`private, max-age=300`). The share card image comes from the private store too, with `Cache-Control: private` (never in the CDN). Leaving link for a narrower level also purges the card.
  - Compose picker and the author's "Who can see" menu offer five choices.
  - The compose picker now opens downward on wide screens (five choices did not fit above the card at the top of the page) and upward in the phone bottom sheet as before.
  - A long choice name now shrinks with "…" instead of pushing "Post" past the card. This also fixes the English "Followers only" overflow from v1.0.93.
- **Media route:** the checked route `/p/{slug}/media/…` now serves live posts of active accounts only. Before, a removed post's media could still be fetched there by a signed-in viewer who knew the key.
- **Admin console (`/admin/posts`):**
  - A "Visibility" column (icon + name). On live posts the value is a button that opens the change form: one of five values plus a required reason. The Actions column is unchanged.
  - Same flow as the author's change: media move, CDN purge, and `visibility_changed` to the backend for NiceNote shares.
  - Audited as `visibility` with `from>to`; the audit page shows "Public → Anyone with the link".
  - Removed / deleted posts keep their setting; read-only members cannot change it.
- **NiceNote / NiceMatrix source API (additive; existing clients keep working):**
  - `PATCH /posts/{id}` accepts `audience` (public / link / followers / friends / private). It sets the exact value and wins over the legacy `visibility`. This is meant for the m.nicematrix.com admin console and, later, the app.
  - `POST /posts` accepts `audience` (missing → public; a repeated request never changes it).
  - Link posts report `visibility: "private"` + `audience: "link"`; the legacy `private` keeps any non-public post as it is.
  - Documented in `docs/api/nicenote.md`; the backend work is in `docs/plan/cross-agent-requests.md` #7.
- **Terms and privacy policy, all 40 languages:** effective dates bumped to 2026-11-23.
  - Terms: the summary and 4.5 list "anyone with the link". 4.5 says such a post is listed nowhere, but anyone with its link can open it without an account and pass the link on, and other platforms may show its preview. Other non-public posts still have no preview.
  - Privacy: a new paragraph on link posts (images and audio included, previews on other platforms).
- **i18n:** 6 new keys × 40 locales.

## Database migrations
- `0027_post_link_visibility`: widens the `post.visibility` check (adds `link`) and the `moderation_action.action` check (adds `visibility`). No row changes. Down: link posts become only-me, admin visibility rows are removed from the audit log.
