## Changed
- Phone post page (< 768 px): "⋯" (author's menu / report) moves into the fixed bottom bar, at its end; the row under the post is gone entirely (no lone "⋯", no empty space). Bar menus (⋯, repost) open upward, clear of the bar. Share is icon-only in the bar (label kept as aria-label / title) so six actions fit at 320 px in every language. Wider screens unchanged.

Private post page shows like + comments + ⋯ (bar on phones, row on wide screens), the comment box, like / reply on comments (no report); the private card on the author's own profile shows like. Reposts, bookmarks, sharing and reports stay public-only.
- Fixes: private posts on phones had no comment button (hidden since v1.0.75 because they had no bottom bar).
