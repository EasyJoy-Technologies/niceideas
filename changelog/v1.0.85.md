## Added
- `ANALYTICS_GA4_ID` / `ANALYTICS_CLARITY_ID` (env, validated; empty = off). Both empty: no banner, no consent script, CSP unchanged — staging and local stay as before unless the ids are set.
- `src/server/analytics/analytics.ts`: configured ids and the vendor CSP sources (`script-src`, `connect-src`; `img-src` already allows https). Added to the page CSP only when a tool is configured.
- Consent banner (`src/server/share/consent.ts`, `public/scripts/consent.js`, `public/styles/consent.css`): hidden until `consent.js` finds no stored choice; Reject and Accept have equal weight; link to privacy §5. Choice in `ni_consent` (180 days, first-party). Nothing third-party loads before Accept; then gtag.js (Consent Mode: analytics granted, all ad signals denied; Google signals and ad personalisation off; cookie lifetime 13 months) and Clarity (`consentv2`: analytics granted, ads denied).
- Clarity masks the whole page: `data-clarity-mask="True"` on `<body>` (enforced in code, independent of the project's masking setting).
- Not offered: settings page (`analytics: false`), admin console (own shell), mainland-China visitors (restricted chrome).
- Privacy page: "Cookie settings" button next to the effective date reopens the banner. Withdrawing tells Clarity, deletes `_ga*` / `_clck` / `_clsk` (host and parent domains) and reloads.
- i18n: `consent.{title,text,accept,reject,settings}`, 5 keys × 40 locales (ro `consent.accept` "Accept" is native Romanian, allow-listed).
