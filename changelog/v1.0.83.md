# v1.0.83 — 2026-10-27

Feed card hover and article spacing, round 2 (Xianglin 2026-10-27 15:18 / 15:31: spacing option B, new hover).

## Changed
- Card hover (mouse only), the same for every feed card: the card lifts — near-white tint, soft purple ring, deeper shadow (`--ni-color-card-hover`, `--ni-shadow-card-hover`, light and dark). Before, short cards turned grey (#f3f4f6), darker than the page, so their top and the avatar edge blended into the background.
- Articles open their post when the card is clicked or tapped anywhere outside links, buttons, menus and media, like short posts (`data-href` on every feed card). A framed article box still turns purple, now while the card is hovered.
- Articles without a cover: no frame at all (the purple hover frame is gone); text laid out like a short post's text — same gap under the author row (≈12 px to the title ink), ≈16 px from the facts line to the action icons (a short post: ≈19 px; 3 px less because the light 12 px facts line reads airier).
