# v1.0.73 — 2026-10-26

F13 from the sharing review: worker leases are fenced, so several worker processes can run side by side safely (plan `docs/plan/2026-10-26-f13-lease-fencing.md`; handoff `nicematrix-system/handoffs/2026-10-26-niceideas-f13-lease-fencing.md`; Xianglin 2026-10-26 16:21).

## Fixed
- A media job, backend notification or avatar job whose lease ran out and was claimed again by another worker could still renew, settle, retry or release the new claim (R-N06). Every claim now gets a lease token; its later writes apply only while the token holds the lease, and a lost lease is logged as `lease_lost` and dropped (no share refresh, no success log).
- Media: a failed lease renewal (every 60 s) aborts the download / conversion; nothing more is stored (objects written meanwhile are removed with their upload).
- Notifications are claimed one at a time (still ≤ 20 per round, lease 120 s); a whole round of slow sends (15 s each) can no longer let the last items fall due to another worker before they are sent. Due items go oldest first.
- Avatars: the profile is pointed at a stored avatar only while the claim holds its lease; otherwise the stored object is deleted again (no orphan in the public bucket).
- Account events: releasing a failed attempt no longer clears the lease of a delivery that took the event over.

## Unchanged
- Single worker: schedule, retry delays, timeouts and log lines as before (`lease_lost` should never appear). Share refresh, CDN purge and media visibility leases already had tokens (tests added).
- Production keeps one worker until `lease_lost` has stayed at 0 for a while; only then may it scale out.

## Tests
- `lease-fencing` (stale claim writes nothing, per queue; refresh / CDN purge / account-event tokens), `lease-runners` (lost lease stops media storing, avatar orphan removed, outbox round under 15 s sends), `lease-stress` (two worker processes, one PostgreSQL, 100 media jobs + 100 notifications + 100 avatars with leases taken over: every item ends once and correctly, stores hold exactly the used objects). 241 tests pass; repro R-N06 no longer reproduces.
