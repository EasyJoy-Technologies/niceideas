Private posts: a post can be made visible only to its author and public again, on the web and from NiceNote (plan `docs/plan/2026-10-26-private-theme-guidelines.md` C; Xianglin 2026-10-26 17:02: others see "not found"; no third-party API yet; handoff `niceideas/handoffs/2026-10-26-niceideas-private-posts-and-theme.md`).

## Added
- `post.visibility` (`public` / `private`, independent of status) + `visibility_changed_at` (migration 0021).
- Private posts: page, share cards (og), likes, comments and reports answer 404 to everyone but the author (a deleted private post stays 404, never 410); left out of timelines, Discover, search, suggested authors / hot articles, other people's view of the profile and its post count, reply notifications of others. Media leave the public store at once and are purged from the CDN edge (existing visibility check + F22 queue); share cards purged too.
- The author still sees the post: "Only you can see this post…" note, noindex, no share card, likes / share hidden, comments read-only; on their own profile the card shows "Only me". Media through the author-only route `/p/{slug}/media/{key}` (private store, single byte ranges for audio seeking, `private, max-age=300`; media public through another post redirect to the public domain). The edit page uses the same URLs.
- Author's "⋯" menu: Make visible only to me / Make public (no-JS form → `POST /api/web/posts/visibility`, same-origin, author only; failure notice `?n=visibility_failed`).
- Public again: media moved back before the change commits (no broken images); old place in feeds (published_at unchanged); nobody notified.
- NiceNote: `PATCH /api/internal/v1/nicenote/posts/{id}` accepts `visibility`; every `post` object has `visibility`; a web change queues the post event `visibility_changed` (`details.visibility`), a backend change sends none.
- Every publishing path keeps a private post's media private: app syncs / web edits, NiceNote pushes and media refresh, moderation restore, cancelled account deletion.
- Storage: `ObjectStore.read(key, range)` (local + S3).
- Terms of use (§1 summary, §4.1, new §4.5) and privacy policy (summary, §4) in 40 locales: posts are public unless made visible only to the author. New UI texts in 40 locales.
