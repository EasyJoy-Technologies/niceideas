# v1.0.14 — 2026-10-20

## Changes
- Post-page interactions (plan §16.2, approved v2 design). Everything here also works in mainland China (new access class `interaction`):
  - Likes on posts and comments by any visitor, deduplicated per browser. The browser is identified by an HttpOnly cookie `ni_vid`, stored only as a keyed hash. Likes are limited per IP.
  - Anonymous comments with an optional name. Comments show at once and are moderated afterwards (Q4). There is one reply level. Comment text is shown as plain text, so links are not clickable. Abuse guards: same-origin check, browser and IP bans, rate limits per browser and per IP, a 2000-character limit, and a single-use proof of work (no third-party captcha).
  - Reporting a post or comment, at most one open report per reporter and target.
  - Action bar with like, comment count, share and report; a fixed bottom bar on phones; comments are paged 30 at a time, oldest first, without needing JS.
- Author controls in the first-party API: `GET /api/v1/posts/{id}/comments`, `DELETE /api/v1/posts/{id}/comments/{commentId}`, and `commentsMode` (`open | signed_in | closed`) in the post settings `PATCH`. Post DTOs now include `likeCount`, `commentCount` and `commentsMode`.
- Moderation CLI `dist/moderate.mjs`: list reports and comments; hide, restore or delete comments; ban or unban commenters; set a post's comment mode; remove or restore posts; resolve or dismiss reports. Every action is audited in `moderation_action`.
- Daily maintenance `dist/maintain.mjs`, run by a systemd timer:
  - physically deletes posts past their 30-day retention, including revisions, comments and likes;
  - deletes media that has been unused for 7 days, objects first;
  - retries failed public-bucket withdrawals;
  - drops expired idempotency keys and bans.
- 25 new interaction strings in all 40 languages.
- New env vars: `INTERACTION_SECRET` (required on staging and production), `COMMENT_POW_BITS`, and `RATE_*` limits for interactions.

## Database migrations
- `0002_interactions`: `post.like_count / comment_count / comments_mode`, `post_like`, `comment`, `comment_like`, `visitor_ban`, `report.comment_id / reporter_key / ip_hash`, and new `moderation_action` actions.
