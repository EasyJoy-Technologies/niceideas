# v1.0.80 — 2026-10-27

Phone footer and feed-card polish (plan `docs/plan/2026-10-27-repost-profile-tabs.md`, items 1, 3–6; Xianglin 2026-10-27 12:06 / 12:23 / 12:29).

## Changed
- Phone footer, two kinds only. With links (overseas feed, profile, bookmarks, post pages): logo + tagline, About · Terms · Privacy · Help edge to edge and evenly spread, ©. Without links (information pages — their links are in the header menu — and mainland China): logo + tagline, ©. Language and theme are no longer repeated in the footer (they are in the header). Languages whose four links do not fit one row get two rows of two, each edge to edge (share.js; without JS they wrap). The tagline is dropped when it would not fit next to the logo on one line. Wide screens unchanged.
- Short posts in lists open their post when tapped anywhere outside links, buttons, menus and media (text selection, Ctrl/⌘/Shift and middle click handled); mouse hover tints the card grey.
- Articles in lists: "Read more ›" ends the facts line; without a cover image the article box has no visible frame (its text lines up with the post text) and shows the purple frame on hover, like framed boxes.
- Bookmark icon is a star everywhere (cards, post page, phone action bar, left column, account menu).
- Menus never wrap their items: a menu is as wide as its longest item (screen width at most, then …); the delete confirmation still wraps inside it. Before, "Make visible only to me" took 2–3 lines in en / de / ru / fr.
- UI text (40 locales): `feed.readMore`.
