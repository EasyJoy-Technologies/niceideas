# v1.0.44 — 2026-10-21

## Performance
- Home timeline (signed in): reads the newest posts of each followed author through `post_author_published_idx` and merges them, instead of filtering the global timeline. At 100k posts / 10k users the query drops from ~37 ms to ~3 ms; cost now depends on the number of followed authors, not on how rare their posts are.
- Feed conditions (live, kind / cover tab, keyset cursor) shared by all feeds in one helper.
