# v1.0.99 — 2026-11-23

Posts with images or audio only (no title, no text) get a name on the public pages too (Xianglin 2026-11-23 15:53: as proposed).

## Why
- `cardTitle()` uses only the title and the summary text, so such a post had an empty name. Checked on staging with an image-only article, an audio-only article, an image-only short post and a quote of the image article (@niceideas_e2e_02): the notification's post link had no text (could not be seen or clicked); Popular articles and More articles by the author showed only "author · date"; the page `<title>` was "· niceideas" and `og:title` / `itemprop=name` / `og:image:alt` were empty (chat previews, native share); feed cards and quote boxes had an empty heading.

## Changes
- **(No text), same as the admin pages:** where the post's name is the text of a link (notifications, Popular articles, More articles by the author) a muted "(No text)" stands in (`.ni-none`, secondary text colour, normal weight; follows the link colour on hover). One shared key `share.post.noText` (moved from `admin.posts.noText`, same wording in all 40 languages); the admin pages use it too through `share/post-label.ts`. "(No content)" stays admin-only (public pages never show such posts).
- **No empty headings:** feed cards (articles) and quote boxes leave the heading out when the post has no title; the cover and the "Article · 1 image" line say what it is.
- **Share title = author:** page title, `og:title`, `og:image:alt`, `itemprop=name` and the native share title use "Name (@handle)" (as the profile page) when the post has no title and no text.
